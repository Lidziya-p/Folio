Warmeston

Warmeston is a wood pellet manufacturer in Estonia, converting forest and wood industry residues into energy efficient biofuel. They manage their business via a platform that integrates data from different phases of procurement - suppliers, logistics of raw materials and monitoring the supplies on the storage field and amount levels in silos. My role was to redesign the platform used by Warmeston and its clients, with a focus on enhancing the overall user experience.

An earthy palette in a data platform

My goal was to create a clean, professional interface that still reflected Warmeston’s rich, earthy color palette. These tones were subtly integrated into navigation, data visualisations and upsell pages - maintaining brand consistency without distracting from the platform’s core functionality.

Registration and contract update

Warmeston needed a flow for registration and annual contract renewal, quite specific to the industry. Every year a supplier has to review their certificates, specify source materials origin and specify the amount of by-products that will be produced the upcoming year.

A step for the upsell of the additional services was also included into the flow, because it was not conveyed anywhere before and was overlooked by clients.

Dashboard

The new dashboard includes the most important information from other pages, providing a quick overview of what is happening in the silos and how full they are, and showing whether production is keeping up with the amounts set as goals.

If there is a yearly form that needs to be filled in, it is also available as a visually prominent block at the top of the dashboard.

Other important blocks for suppliers include ordering additional services, starting to sell additional products, and contacts of the Warmeston support specialists assigned to a particular supplier.

Selling of products

A separate page dedicated to all the by-products Warmeston is currently buying, so that every client can see additional business opportunities, calculate the income and request a quote from Warmeston.

Translating data

The most challenging part of the project was understanding and translating industry-specific data into meaningful visuals. How to visualise target production amounts a client has to meet according to their quarterly contract? How to display sensor data about silo capacity and remaining volume? How to convey the process of emptying a silo and the frequency with which the pick up of sawdust and other by-products occurs?

Those were some of many questions that we had to tackle to create a platform covering a user’s needs.

Wet sawdust

The page provides information only about wet sawdust production. One can get a detailed overview of how much the production is in line with the yearly and monthly goals, and immediately access cameras installed inside the containers and switch between them.

Dry sawdust

The page shows the amount of dry sawdust currently collected in silos, so that Warmeston workers can collect it on time, before the silo limit is reached. A client can monitor when the sawdust was taken away.
