Helge

Helge is an Estonian non-profit organisation dedicated to monitoring and supporting the mental wellbeing of students. Students fill in a short wellbeing questionnaire in the free, ad-free Helge mobile app and get instant personal feedback, while school support specialists see anonymous class and school reports on a dashboard - turning wellbeing data into informed decisions. Today Helge is used by 35 schools and over 9,700 students across Estonia.
