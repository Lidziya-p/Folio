PXL

PXL is a platform designed to support film production teams during film shooting. It allows production teams to create and manage callsheets, send them to everyone involved in filming that day, and review timecards of actors and crew members. The platform helps teams coordinate people and keep essential production information organised and accessible.

Flexible table

The main and most important function of PXL is creating callsheets for film production. A callsheet is essentially a large table created for each project that can be accessed by multiple collaborators. It works as a schedule that allows everyone involved to see when they need to be present on set each day.

It is also used to check whether people involved in the shoot have submitted their timecards on time, which is important for making sure they get paid on time.

I designed the table to be flexible, with a fixed header and collapsible additional information columns, such as status and rate, giving the calendar section more space when needed. Submitted timecards are indicated by a green calendar icon in the corner of each day cell, while a red icon indicates that a timecard was not submitted on time.

To make working with the table more efficient, I added bulk actions in a fixed floating bar that could act on multiple items at once.

Many actions, one table

The table is heavily packed with actions related to team members. Users can edit their role, status, rate and other details, as well as indicate whether a person is working on set on a particular day.

The statuses can vary between projects, and it was impossible to anticipate every possible case, so I added an option to create custom statuses when the required one is not available.

Calendar with a challenge

The calendar was visually challenging because I had to find a clear way to show different stages of a project, as well as substages that could be added within each stage. This was important because multiple teams could be working simultaneously during the same stage.

A calendar per person

An individual calendar for each team member can be seen in their profile. This way, a user can get a full overview of when that person was present on set and which timecards they submitted.

Callsheets and production reports

All generated callsheets and reports are kept separately so that everyone involved in managing a film shoot can get a clear overview of them and their statuses. Callsheets are generated before each shooting day and sent out to all the team members who need to be present on set, including the time they are expected to arrive.

Reports are finalised at the end of the day, and include extra fields for the time each team member left work and took their lunch break. All the data can be edited individually or entered using bulk actions.

Time cards

Cast and crew members can log in and get an overview of their timecards. Because of the specifics of their work, timecards are available on both desktop and mobile.

When a user fills in a timecard for the first time, they have to provide their contact information and legal details, which are then stored in their account. They don’t have to enter their work hours because the data is automatically taken from callsheets and production reports, but they can still review and edit it if needed.

If they used a car for work or rented something for the production, they can add the relevant information and receipts to the timecard to request reimbursement.

Designed for the set

I designed mobile versions of the list and detail pages because callsheets and reports had to be viewed and edited on the go - at shooting locations, in public transport or other public places.
