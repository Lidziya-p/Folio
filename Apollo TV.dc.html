Apollo TV

Apollo TV is an Estonian digital platform with a variety of local and foreign content - over 50 live channels and a VOD library. The client needed a new website that communicated their service better to unregistered users, plus a new onboarding flow with understandable pricing.

Enriched visual language

Apollo TV wanted their visual language improved - at that time it consisted of their logo and a defined typeface. I came up with a visual direction that conveys the atmosphere of cosy movie nights and relaxation, using dark backgrounds, blurring and gradients.

Subscription flow

Apollo TV needed a website that would communicate their service and clarify how people could subscribe to it. Previously, people ended up immediately on the platform without any indication of differently priced packages, offers and additional services. I created a new subscription flow with a services overview.
{{ counter }}

Usability testing

I ran usability testing with 7 users to check the subscription flow and how well they understood the subscription options and available add-ons.

After four sessions I noticed recurring issues: users struggled to understand subscription packages and additional services, felt overwhelmed by the number of devices and options, and weren’t sure whether they had selected everything correctly.

I tweaked the flows for the next rounds of testing, which led to a more understandable solution and a simplified flow.
