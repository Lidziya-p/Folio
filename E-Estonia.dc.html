E-Estonia Briefing Centre

e-Estonia inspires global policy makers, political leaders, corporate executives, investors, and international media with the success story of e-Estonia, guides them on the digitalisation paths and builds links to leading IT-service providers. DUX part was to create new website UX & UI design but also set together with client the communication of the story and structuring of content.

Low-fidelity wireframing

I worked with insights from interviews with e-Estonia’s target audiences, conducted by my colleagues. Based on these findings, I created low-fidelity wireframes focusing on content hierarchy, page structure and overall website logic.

Implementing Estonian brand guidelines

Estonia provides branding guidelines for presenting Estonia in external and internal communication. I had to use their design system when I moved into UI visualisation.

You can check them from here.

Visual exploration

During the UI design phase I created several colour variants for our client to choose from, because Estonian guidelines offer a great variety of colour options that can set a totally different tone and mood of the website.

After the colour palette was confirmed, I created all the remaining pages and responsive designs for tablet and mobile.

Desktop designs were made into a prototype that was used by my colleagues for user testing.
